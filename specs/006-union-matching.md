# 006 — Union matching (marriage-minded) one-pager

**Status:** outline  
**Date:** 2026-07-27  
**Constitution:** §VIII  
**Frame (locked):** 5A — serious dating + logistics; **not** passport retail  

## Intent

Match people who want **real**, non-sham relationships, including those open to legal marriage and relocation. Platform provides discovery, safety tools, and optional logistics (meetup, concierge, housing checklist). **No guarantee** of residency or citizenship from any match or marriage.

## Positioning

| Like | Unlike |
|------|--------|
| Tinder/Hinge with serious-intent filters | “Buy a spouse for a passport” |
| Optional undrlla concierge for in-person meetup | Immigration law firm warranty |
| Checklist of **state** marriage docs (links, not advice-as-product) | Coaching fictitious cohabitation |

## FR sketch

- **FR-U01**: Opt-in profile: intent (`serious` | `marriage_minded` | `open`), countries of interest, languages.  
- **FR-U02**: Mutual match before messaging (or TG deep-link in MVP).  
- **FR-U03**: Hard disclaimers on every funnel step (no citizenship guarantee).  
- **FR-U04**: Report / block / ban; no citizen veto over third-party matches (CONSTITUTION).  
- **FR-U05**: Optional bookable logistics Services (escort meetup, translator) via `001` booking.  
- **FR-U06**: Prohibited content: offers to arrange sham marriage for money.  

## Safety (L1)

- Rate limits, photo moderation later, age 18+ only.  
- Trafficking pattern detection = ops + ToS, not growth metric.  
- Store minimal sensitive prefs; FLS for private fields.

## MVP cut

**Community / TG-first** possible before full app: channel + form + human intro by founder. Full product after housing MVP.

## Non-goals

- State marriage registration software.  
- Guaranteed visa outcomes.  
- Undrlla “civil union” as substitute for state marriage (deferred C model).

## Open

- Brand name (undrmatch / undrpair / …).  
- Whether profiles show citizen badge (signal only).  
- Paid boosts — defer.
