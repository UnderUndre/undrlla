## Executive Context & Architecture Overview

`003-housing-donations` is the first Tier-B flagship spec to actually populate the extension-point seam that `001-init-from-directus` shipped (FR-016). It delivers two distinct capabilities:

1. **Donation-funded housing purchase** — a verified applicant's housing need is funded from a donation pool and paid out exclusively through verified escrow / notary / direct service providers, never as raw cash to the recipient. MVP primary verified housing purchase target is **Turkey (TR)** (where real estate purchase provides clear title and investor citizenship path). **Georgia (GE) is strictly used for 1% Small Business OpCo tax setup and orientation, NOT for donation-funded housing acquisitions** (to protect pool funds against residency rejection risks under Georgian Art. 18 / legal volatility). Expansion to further countries (e.g., Panama) is an admin-panel toggle, not a code change.
2. **Concierge services around housing** — the founder (and, post-MVP, additional approved vendors) offer bookable services such as airport pickup and escort to property signing, proxy purchases via a local bank card, rental-handover help, and legal/banking setup. These reuse the Tier-A `Service` / `Booking` machinery from `001` unchanged.

A clean Tier-A instance (per FR-016, User Story 1 of `001`) MUST still contain none of these collections or behaviors by default. The collections below ship as an isolated Directus extension package registered by the flagship instance only.

### Architectural Philosophy: Three Funding Models

This spec recognizes three distinct funding models for housing. Only **Model A** is in scope for `003` MVP; Models B and C are described here for traceability and MUST NOT be implemented in this feature.

1. **Model A — Donation pool → housing (MVP, this spec).** Donors contribute to a pool earmarked for verified housing needs. An applicant submits a `HousingApplication` against a `HousingProgram`; once verified, the application draws a `HousingPoolAllocation`; on approval the funds move into a `HousingEscrow` record that pays the notary / verified escrow agent / verified vendor directly on signing or handover. The recipient receives keys, not cash. This model is the escrow-payout philosophy of FR-011 applied specifically to housing.

2. **Model B — Personal donor balance / co-pilot savings (deferred).** A user accumulates a personal balance on their account from monthly contributions, activity accelerators, or deposits, and spends that balance on their own housing. Requires a `DonorLedger` / `DonorBalance` model and an explicit spend-to-housing flow. **Out of scope for `003`** — listed for traceability.

3. **Model C — Direct payment + commission (deferred).** The buyer pays for the property themselves (fiat / crypto) and the platform takes a commission for the transaction, escrow, and concierge. Effectively treats housing as a high-ticket service purchase. **Out of scope for `003`** — listed for traceability.

### Product intent: housing + path-to-papers as one package story (countries may differ)

The **primary product story** (expected highest demand) is helping people get **housing + orientation toward a legal mobility track** (residency / CBI / naturalization — and thus eventually a passport where law allows) as **one package outcome in marketing and funding** — not separate "citizenship" vs "passport" **donation** products. A passport is an outcome of a **state** process, never an Undrlla guarantee.

**Housing country and papers-track country are independent** (CONSTITUTION §VII.2; research D6b): applicant MAY buy property in country **X** and pursue papers in country **Y**. Pool + escrow fund **housing in X** only; Y is metadata + optional concierge/checklist. Default application target is **USD 500,000**. Country legal minima are advisory metadata on the **papers** track (or housing when tracks are coupled in UX).

### Funding model (locked 2026-07-22): shared pot + queue (not earmarked)

**Model A MVP is a shared kettle, not personal crowdfunding:**

1. **Donors / subscribers** contribute to the **shared `HousingPool`** via **custom recurring subscription** (any amount **≥ USD 1** per billing period; no fixed plan grid) and optional one-off top-ups. Donors do **not** pick a recipient in MVP.
2. **How the pot and queue work together (locked — model B):**
   - **Everyone’s** subscriptions and one-offs feed the **same** `HousingPool` (after 5% fee). Nobody’s money is reserved for themselves.
   - When the person at the **front of the active queue** is funded, the pool pays their full `requested_amount` (if balance ≥ that amount) via allocation → escrow → notary. Active subscribers refill the pot for whoever is first.
   - **Queue order (FIFO):** among **active** eligible applicants, earlier `queue_entered_at` wins.
   - **Enter the queue:** `HousingApplication` reaches `verified` (KYC + proof — **no neediness score**) **and** at least one successful pool contribution ≥ USD 1 **and** an **active monthly subscription** (≥ USD 1/period).  
     `queue_entered_at = max(first_successful_pool_contribution_at, verified_at, subscription_activated_at)` (cannot park a seat before verify + paying sub).
   - **Keep the seat (model B — locked):** place stays **`queue_status=active` only while the housing-pool subscription is active**. One-off alone is **not** enough to remain active after entry rules are met without a live sub.
   - **Payment grace:** failed recurring charge → retry up to **7 days** → then `queue_status=paused` (skipped for allocation). **`queue_entered_at` is preserved** while paused.
   - **Long lapse:** if still not active after **90 days** paused (or sub canceled without restart), `queue_status=left` and a later re-entry gets a **new** `queue_entered_at` (back of the line), unless admin hardship override keeps the old key (audit log).
   - **Resubscribe within 90 days:** return to `active` with the **same** `queue_entered_at`.
   - Allocation requires pool balance ≥ `requested_amount` and amount-approval. If head of **active** queue needs more than balance, **wait** (MVP: do not skip to a smaller applicant behind).
3. **Earmarked donations** deferred post-MVP.
4. Applicant `requested_amount` = need size when funded, not a donor thermometer.

**Donation platform fee (locked):** **5%** + pass-through payment fees (default). No second fee on escrow release.

**Refunds (locked for MVP):** **Housing donation subscriptions and pool one-offs are non-refundable** once payment succeeds (funds already enter fee + pool). UI MUST state this before pay. **Legal exception path:** where mandatory cooling-off / consumer law requires a refund window, handle as rare admin/compliance exception (not self-serve refund button). Chargebacks follow payment-provider dispute process and MUST reverse pool credit / fee ledger when lost.

**Self-serve amount policy:** `requested_amount` **≤ USD 500,000** → `amount_approval_status=auto_approved`. **> USD 500,000** → admin amount-approval before allocation. KYC verify remains a separate gate.

**Explicitly out of scope for `003` (post-`003` roadmap):** in-app rental PM, smart-home; earmarked peer donations; Model B/C as before.

**Housing / CBI professional workforce:** MVP concierge founder-only. Post-MVP multi-vendor lawyers/realtors/etc. (self-signup, ordinary marketplace fee).

### Open dependency on the Mutual Aid Queue spec

The Tier-A spec (`001`) describes the $300k Mutual Aid Queue (MAQ) under FR-009..FR-011 and the `MutualAidQueue` collection, but defers all flagship work to follow-up specs. `003` introduces a **housing-only** `HousingPool` so that this feature can ship independently and auditable before any MAQ spec lands. When a future MAQ spec (`004+`) is authored, the MAQ owner decides whether `HousingPool` (a) merges into the general MAQ as the `need_category='housing'` specialization, or (b) stays a dedicated pool with its own escrow chain. Either resolution is acceptable; **the decision is explicitly deferred to the MAQ spec author** and is logged as a research open-question (see `research.md` D4). `003` MUST be implementable today without that MAQ spec existing.

## User Scenarios & Acceptance Criteria *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 — Admin enables a country and an applicant gets housing funded by the donation pool (Priority: P1)

As a platform admin, I want to control per-country availability of donation-funded property purchase and process a verified applicant end-to-end through escrow payout, so that housing reaches a real person without the platform ever disbursing raw cash to them.

**Why this priority**: This is the core value proposition of the donation-funded housing feature — the entire reason `003` exists. If the admin cannot scope countries or the escrow chain cannot complete, the feature has no MVP.

**Independent Test**: Can be tested by enabling TR in `ServiceArea`, seeding a `HousingProgram` for TR, submitting a `HousingApplication` with proof, verifying and approving it, allocating pool funds, releasing the `HousingEscrow` to a notary destination, and verifying the audit chain `donor → HousingPool → HousingPoolAllocation → HousingApplication → HousingEscrow → destination` is complete and that no raw-cash transfer to the applicant exists anywhere.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a flagship instance with the `003` extension registered, **When** the admin opens the `ServiceArea` admin panel, **Then** TR and GE are present with `feature_scope='housing_purchase'`, `enabled=true`, and at least one other country (e.g. DE) is present with `enabled=false`.
2. **Given** an admin panel with DE disabled for `housing_purchase`, **When** a user attempts to submit a `HousingApplication` for DE, **Then** the system rejects the application at intake with a `country_not_enabled` error and creates no record in `submitted` or later states.
3. **Given** TR enabled and a `HousingProgram` for TR with default `target_amount` USD 500,000 and a configured `payout_destination_type`, **When** an authenticated user submits a `HousingApplication` with proof URLs, a target property reference (optional), and `requested_amount` ≤ 500_000 USD, **Then** the application is created in `submitted` status with `amount_approval_status=auto_approved` and is visible to the applicant and to admins.
4. **Given** the same program, **When** an authenticated user submits a `HousingApplication` with `requested_amount` > 500_000 USD, **Then** the application is created in `submitted` status with `amount_approval_status=pending` and MUST NOT receive a `HousingPoolAllocation` until an admin sets `amount_approval_status=approved`.
5. **Given** a `submitted` application, **When** an admin reviews the proof and marks the applicant verified (including KYC/AML per the open legal dependency), **Then** the application transitions to `verified` and becomes eligible for pool allocation only if `amount_approval_status` ∈ {`auto_approved`, `approved`}.
6. **Given** a `verified` application with amount approved and sufficient `HousingPool` balance, **When** the admin approves it, **Then** a `HousingPoolAllocation` is created for the application's `requested_amount` (not necessarily the program default), debits the pool, credits the application, and the application transitions to `approved`.
7. **Given** an `approved` application with the signing scheduled and the notary destination known, **When** the admin creates the `HousingEscrow`, **Then** the destination (notary bank account / verified escrow agent / verified vendor) is snapshotted on the escrow record at creation time, the amount held equals `requested_amount`, and the application transitions to `funded`.
8. **Given** a `funded` application and a `HousingEscrow` with a snapshotted destination, **When** the notary / verified escrow agent confirms the signing, **Then** the funds move from the escrow to the snapshotted destination (never to the applicant), the escrow transitions to `released`, and the application transitions to `escrow_released` and then `completed`. The audit chain is complete and contains no raw-cash transfer to the applicant.
9. **Given** a destination change after a `HousingEscrow` is created, **When** a new application is approved, **Then** the new application's escrow uses the new destination while the original escrow keeps its original snapshot.
10. **Given** a campaign UI for a `program_type` covering housing+CBI, **When** a donor or applicant views the goal labels, **Then** there is a single package goal (housing + citizenship-by-investment) and no separate "passport" fundraiser line.

### User Story 2 — Founder publishes concierge services and a customer books one (Priority: P2)

As the founder (and sole MVP concierge provider), I want to publish bookable concierge services around housing (escort to signing, proxy purchases via my local card, rental-handover help, legal/banking setup), so that a housing applicant can book the help they need through the same booking flow they already use for any other service.

**Why this priority**: Concierge services are the differentiator that makes donation-funded housing actually work operationally — without escort and proxy-purchase help, many recipients cannot complete a property purchase in a foreign country. P2 because US1 (the funding chain) is the gating value, but US2 ships in the same MVP.

**Independent Test**: Can be tested by seeding the founder as a vendor under the founder tenant, creating four `Service` rows (one per concierge offering, each with `provider_id` = founder), having a customer select a slot and create a `pending` `Booking`, the founder confirming it, and — for a pre-paid service — a linked `PaymentAttempt` being captured on confirmation. All of this uses the Tier-A `Service`/`Booking`/`PaymentAttempt` machinery unchanged.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the founder is an approved vendor with `provider_id` under the founder tenant, **When** the founder creates a `Service` titled "Escort to property signing (TR)", **Then** the service is visible in the catalog with the founder as provider, bookable by customers, and (optionally) linked via `ServiceArea` to TR only.
2. **Given** the four MVP concierge services (escort-to-signing, proxy-purchases, rental-handover, legal/banking) created as `Service` rows, **When** a customer views the catalog filtered to the founder tenant, **Then** all four are listed and each can be booked independently through the standard `Booking` flow (FR-019/FR-030).
3. **Given** a pre-paid concierge `Service` and a customer-selected slot, **When** the customer requests a booking, **Then** a `pending` `Booking` is created with a linked `PaymentAttempt` (card authorized or crypto charged per FR-030); **When** the founder confirms, **Then** payment is captured and the slot is atomically reserved; **When** the founder rejects, **Then** the card authorization is voided or the crypto charge auto-refunded.
4. **Given** a concierge `Service` linked to TR via `ServiceArea`, **When** a customer attempts to book it for a non-TR context, **Then** the system refuses the booking with `service_not_available_in_area`; **Given** the same service with no `ServiceArea` link, **When** a customer books it, **Then** the booking is allowed regardless of area.
5. **Given** a customer with an active `HousingApplication`, **When** they book the founder's "Escort to property signing" service, **Then** the booking and the application are visible together in the customer's dashboard (read-only cross-reference; the booking does not mutate the application lifecycle).

## Edge Cases

- **What happens when a country is disabled mid-application?** The existing application is allowed to complete its current lifecycle stage but MUST NOT advance; a `country_disabled` flag is surfaced to applicant and admin; new applications for that country are rejected at intake (FR-002).
- **What happens when the `HousingPool` is insufficient to cover an approved application?** The application is parked in `approved` (not `funded`) until the pool refills; a partial-allocation path is **out of scope for MVP** (FR-006 records full-amount allocation only). The admin sees a `pool_insufficient` indicator.
- **What happens when `requested_amount` > USD 500,000 and amount is not yet admin-approved?** The application MAY progress through intake and KYC to `verified`, but MUST NOT receive a `HousingPoolAllocation` or enter `approved`/`funded` until `amount_approval_status=approved`. If the admin rejects the amount, the applicant MAY lower `requested_amount` to ≤ 500_000 (auto-approved) or resubmit another amount for review.
- **What happens when the applicant later raises `requested_amount` above 500k after auto-approval?** Raising above the self-serve cap resets `amount_approval_status` to `pending` and blocks allocation until re-approved. Lowering an already-approved amount is allowed without re-approval as long as the new amount is ≤ the previously approved amount or ≤ 500k.
- **Does USD 500k guarantee citizenship in the chosen country?** No. Country CBI legal minima are advisory metadata. An application MAY fund housing below a country's CBI threshold; citizenship eligibility is operational/legal, not a system-enforced hard block in MVP (warn-only if `advisory_cbi_min` is set and `requested_amount` is below it).
- **Can housing country ≠ papers country?** Yes. Escrow and property are always for **housing_country**. `papers_track_country` / `papers_track_type` may differ; disabled or incomplete papers metadata yields `papers_track_warn`, not intake rejection.
- **What happens when a notary / escrow agent rejects or the signing falls through?** The `HousingEscrow` transitions to `failed_release` rather than `released`; funds remain held or are returned to the `HousingPool` per a configurable policy (MVP default: return to pool). The application reverts to `approved` for re-scheduling; it does NOT silently complete.
- **What happens when the destination changes after escrow creation?** The existing `HousingEscrow` keeps its snapshot; only new escrows use the new destination (mirrors `PaymentAttempt.payout_wallet_snapshot` semantics, FR-025).
- **What happens on duplicate or fraudulent applications?** The system MUST detect duplicate applications by the same `applicant_user_id` against the same or a substantially-identical target property and surface them to the admin for manual review before verification; verified fraud blocks the applicant from further submissions pending admin action.
- **What happens to concierge slot overlaps?** Standard `Booking` overlap rules from FR-019 apply unchanged: multiple `pending` requests may coexist, confirming one atomically rejects overlapping `pending` requests.
- **What happens when a concierge vendor is unavailable long-term?** Standard `Booking` cancel/reschedule from FR-019 applies; no housing-specific behavior. Post-MVP multi-vendor concierge mitigates single-vendor outage risk.
- **What about exchange-rate volatility on a long escrow hold (e.g. Turkish lira)?** The `HousingEscrow` records a settlement currency and, where relevant, a rate snapshot at creation; long holds are flagged as an operational risk in `research.md`. MVP does not implement automatic re-pegging.
- **What about PII in property addresses, applicant identity documents, notary identities, and bank-account destinations?** All are sensitive operational PII under the FR-036 principle from `001`, extended by FR-011 in this spec: role-masked, audit-redacted, retention-bounded. Vendors never see another vendor's or another applicant's PII; customers see only their own.
- **What happens if `003` is registered on a Tier-A-only client instance by mistake?** Per FR-016 of `001`, a clean Tier-A instance MUST contain none of the `003` collections by default; the extension package is opt-in at the instance level. Registering it is an explicit admin/operational action.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: System MUST provide a universal `ServiceArea` collection for admin-controlled per-country (and optional per-region) availability of features. Each `ServiceArea` record MUST carry `country` (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2), optional `region`, a `feature_scope` discriminator (e.g. `housing_purchase`, `papers_track`, `concierge_service`, and — reserved for future Tier-B specs — `delivery`, `taxi`, `warehouse`, `barter`, `marketplace_directory`), an `enabled` boolean, an optional `tenant_id` (for tenant-scoped features), free-form `notes`, and optional `metadata` JSON. Adding or removing a country for any feature MUST be a data-only admin action requiring no code change or redeploy.
- **FR-002**: System MUST gate `HousingApplication` intake by **housing** `ServiceArea` only. An application whose **housing** country has no enabled `ServiceArea` where `feature_scope='housing_purchase'` MUST be rejected at intake with `country_not_enabled` and create no record in `submitted` or later states. If the **housing** country is disabled after an application is created, the application MUST NOT advance past its current status and MUST surface a `country_disabled` flag to the applicant and admin. A missing or disabled `papers_track` ServiceArea MUST NOT alone reject intake; the system MUST set `papers_track_warn` instead.
- **FR-003**: System MUST provide a `HousingProgram` collection describing a housing-purchase program: `id`, `tenant_id`, `country` (M2O `ServiceArea` with `feature_scope='housing_purchase'` — housing template country), optional `default_papers_track_country` / `default_papers_track_type`, `program_type` (MUST include at least `housing_cbi` for the flagship "housing + path-to-papers" package story; other types e.g. `housing_need`, `residency_only` MAY exist), `target_amount` (platform default **500000** for `housing_cbi`), `currency` (default `USD`), `self_serve_max_amount` (default **500000**, same units as `target_amount`), optional `advisory_cbi_min` (nullable advisory minimum for papers track — advisory only), `eligibility_rules` (structured JSON), `payout_destination_type` (`notary` | `escrow_agent` | `verified_vendor`), and `status`. A `HousingProgram` MUST NOT be active for a **housing** country whose `housing_purchase` ServiceArea is disabled. Program marketing MUST treat housing+path-to-papers as **one package story**; it MUST NOT present separate donation goals for "citizenship" vs "passport".
- **FR-025** *(Housing ⟂ papers)*: System MUST allow `HousingApplication.papers_track_country` and `papers_track_type` to differ from the housing country. Pool allocation and `HousingEscrow` MUST apply only to the **housing** purchase path. UI and API MUST NOT claim that funding guarantees state citizenship or that X and Y must match. Optional `papers_same_as_housing` (default true) MAY prefill papers from housing; applicant MAY decouple.
- **FR-004**: System MUST provide a `HousingApplication` collection and a status state machine `draft → submitted → verified → approved → funded → escrow_released → completed | rejected`. Fields: `id`, `program_id` (M2O `HousingProgram`), `applicant_user_id` (M2O Directus user), `country` / `housing_country` (housing), `papers_same_as_housing`, `papers_track_country`, `papers_track_type`, optional `papers_track_warn`, `status`, `requested_amount` (positive decimal; housing need size when allocated), `amount_approval_status` (`auto_approved` | `pending` | `approved` | `rejected`), `proof_urls`, `target_property_ref` (nullable; property in housing country), and timestamp fields per transition. On create/update of `requested_amount`: if `requested_amount` ≤ program `self_serve_max_amount` (default 500000 USD), system MUST set `amount_approval_status=auto_approved`; if `requested_amount` > `self_serve_max_amount`, system MUST set `amount_approval_status=pending` until an admin sets `approved` or `rejected`. Only `verified` applications with `amount_approval_status` ∈ {`auto_approved`, `approved`} MAY receive a `HousingPoolAllocation`; only `approved` applications MAY receive a `HousingEscrow`. Allocation and escrow amounts MUST use `requested_amount`, not the program default alone. Duplicate detection by `(applicant_user_id, target_property_ref)` MUST flag to admin before verification (see Edge Cases).
- **FR-005**: System MUST provide a `HousingEscrow` collection that snapshots the payout destination at creation: `id`, `application_id` (M2O `HousingApplication`), `destination_type` (`notary` | `escrow_agent` | `verified_vendor`), `destination_account_ref` (snapshotted bank account / wallet / vendor id), `amount_held`, `currency` (MUST be denominated in a stable hard currency — EUR, USD, or USDT — to protect against local fiat currency volatility during multi-week holds), `status` (`held` | `released` | `failed_release`), `snapshot_at`, `released_at`. Funds MUST move out of escrow only on the `provider_confirmed`-equivalent signal from the notary / verified escrow agent / verified vendor (the destination confirms signing/handover); the applicant MUST NOT be the destination of funds. A destination change after creation applies only to future escrows; existing escrows keep their snapshot (mirrors `PaymentAttempt.payout_wallet_snapshot`, FR-025 of `001`).
- **FR-006**: System MUST provide a `HousingPoolAllocation` collection linking a `HousingApplication` to its funding source: `id`, `application_id` (M2O), `source_pool_id` (M2O `HousingPool`), `amount`, `currency`, `allocated_at`. The allocation MUST debit the source `HousingPool.balance` and MUST be reflected in the audit chain. MVP supports full-amount allocation only equal to the application's `requested_amount`; partial allocations are out of scope (see Edge Cases — pool insufficient). Allocation MUST be refused if `amount_approval_status` ∉ {`auto_approved`, `approved`}.
- **FR-017**: System MUST enforce the self-serve amount policy: `self_serve_max_amount` defaults to **500000 USD** on `housing_cbi` programs; applicants MAY choose any positive `requested_amount` ≤ that cap without amount-admin approval; amounts above the cap require admin amount-approval. Changing `requested_amount` upward across the cap re-opens amount approval (`pending`). Admin UI MUST expose amount-approve / amount-reject actions separately from KYC verify and application approve.
- **FR-018**: Ongoing rental property-management (tenant rent payments in-app, lease lifecycle) and smart-home / keyless access integrations are **out of scope for `003`** and deferred to post-`003` specs. The MVP rental-handover concierge `Service` (one-shot help) remains in scope under FR-008.
- **FR-007**: System MUST provide a `HousingPool` collection for Model A: `id`, `tenant_id`, `balance`, `currency`, `status` (`active` | `paused` | `closed`). The pool balance MUST be debited only through a `HousingPoolAllocation` and credited through an automated Directus event hook listening for completed `001` donation `Order` / subscription payment items **after** deducting the platform donation fee (FR-024). All incoming webhook event credits (Paddle `event_id` or `undrepay` `upi_...` intent id) MUST be processed idempotently using a processed-events deduplication table to guarantee zero double-credits to the pool. The pool is housing-only in `003` and is a **shared pot** — credits MUST NOT be earmarked to a specific `HousingApplication` in MVP. Relationship to a future general Mutual Aid Queue is deferred to the MAQ spec author.
- **FR-024** *(Donation fee)*: System MUST apply a **platform donation fee of 5%** (configurable, default 5%) on each successful donation payment (subscription invoice or one-off) destined for `HousingPool`. Net credit to the pool = `gross_donation * (1 - fee_rate)` minus any configured pass-through payment-rail fees if those are not absorbed in the 5%. The fee portion MUST be recorded on the `Order` / fee ledger (`platform_fee_amount` or equivalent). Donors MUST see fee disclosure before subscribe/pay. Earmarked donations are out of scope for `003`. *(FR-024 avoids collision with `001` FR-020 refunds.)*
- **FR-021** *(Subscription plans)*: Housing-pool contributions MUST support **custom amount ≥ 1.00 USD** per **monthly** recurring period by default; no fixed plan grid. One-off donations ≥ 1.00 USD to the same pool MUST be allowed (feed the pot; do **not** alone keep queue `active` without a live subscription under FR-022). Failed recurring charges MUST retry within **7 days**, then mark subscription past_due/canceled and trigger queue pause per FR-022. System MUST model `HousingPoolSubscription` (data-model).
- **FR-022** *(Queue — model B)*: System MUST maintain a housing allocation queue ordered by ascending `queue_entered_at` among `queue_status=active` rows, where `queue_entered_at = max(first_successful_pool_contribution_at, verified_at, subscription_activated_at)`. To be **`active`**: application `verified` (or later non-terminal pre-funded), **active** housing-pool subscription (≥ 1.00 USD/month), and before **allocation** also `amount_approval_status` ∈ {`auto_approved`,`approved`}. **No neediness score.** On sub failure after 7-day grace → `queue_status=paused` (**preserve** `queue_entered_at`). If not returned to active within **90 days** → `queue_status=left` (re-entry requires new sub + new `queue_entered_at` unless admin hardship override). Resubscribe within 90 days → `active` with **same** `queue_entered_at`. Next to fund = earliest **active** with `requested_amount` ≤ pool balance (MVP: head waits if unaffordable; do not skip). Admin MAY override order with audit log. Applicant UI MUST show non-binding **queue position** among active rows (and optional “waiting for pot balance” when head but underfunded).
- **FR-023** *(No refund + chargeback accounting)*: Successful housing-pool subscription charges and one-off donations MUST be **non-refundable** via self-serve flows. Pre-pay UI MUST disclose non-refundability. Chargebacks/statutory refunds MUST: (1) reverse fee + pool credit on ledger; (2) debit **unallocated** pool balance first; (3) if insufficient, pause pool and alert admin — no auto clawback of released escrows in MVP.
- **FR-008**: System MUST model concierge services as ordinary `Service` records from `001` with `provider_id` set to the founder (MVP) or any future approved vendor. The four MVP offerings — escort-to-signing, proxy-purchases via local bank card, rental-handover, and legal/banking setup — MUST each be a distinct `Service` row, each independently bookable via the standard `Booking` flow (FR-019/FR-030 of `001`). `003` MUST NOT introduce a dedicated concierge collection. A concierge `Service` MAY be linked to one or more `ServiceArea` records with `feature_scope='concierge_service'`; a booking for a service in an area where it is not enabled MUST be refused with `service_not_available_in_area`.
- **FR-019** *(Post-MVP multi-vendor housing workforce — not required to ship founder-only MVP)*: After MVP, system MUST allow providers (lawyers, realtors, translators, document runners, notary-escorts, and similar) to **self-signup** as vendors, complete Stripe Connect / crypto payout onboarding (same pattern as `001` FR-025), and publish housing/CBI-related `Service` rows under their own `provider_id`, discoverable by applicants with sort/filter by **price** in simple form; reviews/ratings MAY follow. Platform fee is the ordinary marketplace fee, not a separate logistics fee. This expands FR-008 without new collections.
- **SCOPE — Deferred (2026-07-21)**: FR-009 and FR-010 below specify Model B (personal donor balance) and Model C (direct payment + commission) that are **out of scope for `003`** and deferred to follow-up specs. They remain listed for traceability only and MUST NOT be implemented in this feature.
- **FR-009**: System MUST support a personal donor balance / co-pilot savings model (Model B) allowing a user to accumulate contributions and spend them on their own housing through the same escrow chain. *(Deferred — see SCOPE block above.)*
- **FR-010**: System MUST support a direct-payment-plus-commission model (Model C) allowing a self-funded buyer to pay directly for a property with the platform taking a commission for transaction, escrow, and concierge. *(Deferred — see SCOPE block above.)*
- **FR-011**: System MUST extend the PII handling principle of FR-036 (`001`) to cover property addresses, applicant identity documents, notary / escrow-agent identities, and destination bank-account / wallet references. These fields MUST use structured validation at API boundaries, role-specific field permissions/masks (admin full read; applicant reads own; vendor reads only what is needed to deliver a booked service; no cross-applicant reads), audit-log redaction (logs MUST NOT contain raw document numbers, full bank accounts, or full wallet addresses — only stable record IDs and short suffixes for support correlation), and documented retention/deletion rules per instance.
- **FR-012**: System MUST apply KYC/AML gating to `HousingApplication.verified` (`kyc_status`) and to `HousingEscrow.destination` (`destination_kyc_status`). An application MUST NOT reach `verified` without recorded applicant KYC (`passed`); an escrow destination MUST NOT be released without recorded destination identity verification (`passed`). The system MUST define structured KYC status fields, document upload slots, and verification audit logs.
  - **MVP KYC mode (locked 2026-07-29): MANUAL / AGENCY — no mandatory third-party API.** Admin (or contracted compliance agency) reviews passport/ID + proof packages offline or via secure document exchange; records `kyc_status=passed|failed|pending` + `kyc_method=manual_admin|manual_agency` + optional `agency_case_ref` + auditor identity. **Do not block `003` implement on Sumsub/Onfido integration.**
  - **Post-MVP:** optional automated provider (Sumsub/Onfido/etc.) MAY plug into the same status fields via a dedicated verification interface; provider choice remains ops decision, not a hard dependency of this feature.
- **FR-013**: Implementation of `003` MUST NOT proceed past the planning artifacts (spec + data-model + research + plan + tasks) without satisfying the constitution's Principle VI Cross-AI Review Gate: `specs/003-housing-donations/reviews/analyze.md` with verdict ∈ {PASS, OVERRIDDEN} AND at least two distinct external-AI reviewer files (`reviews/<provider>.md`, different providers) with verdict ∈ {PASS, OVERRIDDEN}. Override via `--override-gate <reason>` is logged to `reviews/_gate-override.md` with timestamp, actor, commit SHA, and reason. The author of the spec MUST NOT also be an external reviewer of it.
- **FR-014**: System MUST ship all `003` collections as an isolated Directus extension package registered only by flagship instances. A clean Tier-A instance (per FR-016 of `001`, User Story 1 Acceptance Scenario 2) MUST contain none of the `003` collections or behaviors by default; registering the extension is an explicit operator action.
- **FR-015**: System MUST provide an admin UI (Directus Studio surface in MVP; custom UI deferred) for: `ServiceArea` enable/disable per country per feature, `HousingProgram` create/update (including `target_amount`, `self_serve_max_amount`, optional `advisory_cbi_min`), `HousingApplication` verify / amount-approve / amount-reject / approve / reject, `HousingPoolAllocation` creation (manual or auto on approval), and `HousingEscrow` create / release / fail. The admin UI MUST enforce the same row filters and field permissions as the API.
- **FR-016**: System MUST expose applicant-facing flows (apply, status, document upload) and concierge-booking flows through both the `undrllanding` Next.js storefront and the Telegram Mini App from `001`, sharing a single versioned contract delta defined in the plan/tasks phase. The admin surfaces (FR-015) are Directus Studio in MVP.

### Key Entities

- **ServiceArea** (Flagship): A universal per-country / per-region feature-availability toggle (`id`, `country` ISO-3166-1 alpha-2, `region` nullable, `feature_scope` enum (`housing_purchase` | `papers_track` | `concierge_service` | reserved `delivery` | `taxi` | `barter`), `enabled` boolean, `tenant_id` nullable, `notes`, optional `metadata`). The single admin-panel source of truth for "where is feature X available". *(Geo Destination Advisory D6c: Recommended Tier-1 expansion countries for offshore `housing_purchase` & `papers_track` are Cyprus `CY` (€300k PR / Non-Dom 0%), UAE `AE` (2M AED Golden Visa / 0% tax), Panama `PA` ($300k QIV / USD / 0% territorial), Greece `GR` (€250k conversion Golden Visa), Malta `MT` (€375k MPRP), Italy `IT` (€200k Flat Tax Art. 24-bis), and Montenegro `ME` (EUR / EU accession candidate 2028-2030). For primary-residence owner-occupants, top targets are Canada Alberta `CA-AB` (0% LTT; WP ≥183d required), and US North Carolina `US-NC`, Washington `US-WA`, Nevada `US-NV` & Tennessee `US-TN`. Advisory warning gates apply to `TR` due to SPK valuation gaps & seismic risk; `GE` where Batumi is flagged for mold/illiquidity; `US-TX` due to 2.5%/yr property tax drag; `US-FL` due to insurance crisis & SB 264. Spain `ES` and Portugal `PT` are blacklisted due to Golden Visa abolitions & okupas/tax traps; `DE`/`FR`/`NL` blacklisted for key-holding tax traps §8 AO.)*
- **HousingProgram** (Flagship): A housing-purchase program template (`id`, `tenant_id`, `country` M2O housing ServiceArea, optional `default_papers_track_*`, `program_type` e.g. `housing_cbi`, `target_amount` default 500000, `currency` default USD, `self_serve_max_amount` default 500000, optional `advisory_cbi_min`, `eligibility_rules` JSON, `payout_destination_type` (`notary` | `escrow_agent` | `verified_vendor`), `status`).
- **HousingApplication** (Flagship): A verified applicant's request for donation-funded housing under a program (`id`, `program_id` M2O, `applicant_user_id` M2O, `country`/`housing_country`, `papers_same_as_housing`, `papers_track_country`, `papers_track_type`, optional `papers_track_warn`, `status` (`draft` | `submitted` | `verified` | `approved` | `funded` | `escrow_released` | `completed` | `rejected`), `requested_amount`, `amount_approval_status` (`auto_approved` | `pending` | `approved` | `rejected`), `proof_urls`, `target_property_ref` nullable, plus transition timestamps).
- **HousingEscrow** (Flagship): A snapshot of the payout destination with held funds for an approved application (`id`, `application_id` M2O, `destination_type` (`notary` | `escrow_agent` | `verified_vendor`), `destination_account_ref` snapshotted, `amount_held`, `currency`, `status` (`held` | `released` | `failed_release`), `snapshot_at`, `released_at`). The applicant is never the destination.
- **HousingPoolAllocation** (Flagship): A debit from the pool funding an application (`id`, `application_id` M2O, `source_pool_id` M2O `HousingPool`, `amount`, `currency`, `allocated_at`).
- **HousingPool** (Flagship): The Model A donation pool backing housing applications (`id`, `tenant_id`, `balance`, `currency`, `status` (`active` | `paused` | `closed`)). Housing-only in `003`; relationship to a future MAQ is the open dependency in the Executive Context.
- *(Reused from `001` unchanged — no new entity created for concierge)*: **Service** (with `provider_id` = founder or approved vendor), **Booking**, **PaymentAttempt**, **Order**, **OrderSplit**.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: An admin can enable or disable a country for `housing_purchase` via the `ServiceArea` admin panel in under 30 seconds, with the change taking effect on intake immediately and requiring no code change or redeploy.
- **SC-002**: Automated end-to-end tests verify the full Model A audit chain — donor event → `HousingPool` credit → `HousingPoolAllocation` debit → `HousingApplication` advance → `HousingEscrow` hold → destination release — completes with no raw-cash transfer to the applicant anywhere in the record. Allocation and escrow amounts equal the application's `requested_amount`.
- **SC-012**: Automated tests verify that `requested_amount` ≤ `self_serve_max_amount` (default 500000 USD) yields `amount_approval_status=auto_approved` and can reach allocation after verify; `requested_amount` > cap yields `pending` and blocks allocation until admin amount-approval; raising amount above the cap re-opens `pending`.
- **SC-013**: Campaign / program surfaces for `housing_cbi` present a single housing+CBI package goal (default target 500000 USD) and do not expose a separate "passport" donation goal.
- **SC-003**: Automated tests verify that a `HousingEscrow` snapshots its destination at creation, that a destination change after creation affects only future escrows, and that the original escrow's snapshot is immutable for the life of that escrow.
- **SC-004**: Automated tests verify that `HousingApplication` intake rejects a disabled **housing** country with `country_not_enabled` (no record created) and that disabling a housing country mid-application parks the application with a `country_disabled` flag without losing prior state.
- **SC-014**: Automated tests verify an application may set `papers_track_country` ≠ housing country; intake still succeeds when housing is enabled; escrow/property fields remain housing-scoped; disabled papers track alone does not reject intake (warn only).
- **SC-005**: Automated tests verify that the four MVP concierge services are creatable as ordinary `Service` rows with the founder as `provider_id`, are independently bookable via the standard `Booking` flow, and that pre-paid concierge bookings capture payment on confirm and void/refund on reject exactly as FR-030 of `001` specifies — without any concierge-specific code path.
- **SC-006**: Automated tests verify that a concierge `Service` linked to a `ServiceArea` with `feature_scope='concierge_service'` refuses bookings outside its area with `service_not_available_in_area`, while the same service with no area link is bookable globally.
- **SC-007**: 100% of `003` API endpoints respect Directus policy/permission row filters and field permissions; cross-applicant reads of PII (property address, identity docs, escrow destination) are blocked; vendors see only the PII strictly required to deliver their own booked service.
- **SC-008**: A clean Tier-A instance (no `003` extension registered) contains none of the `003` collections and exhibits none of the `003` behaviors — verified by the same User Story 1 Acceptance Scenario 2 test as `001`.
- **SC-009**: The constitution's Principle VI Cross-AI Review Gate is satisfied before any implementation task begins: `analyze.md` exists with verdict PASS (or OVERRIDDEN with logged reason), and at least two distinct external-AI reviewer files exist with verdict PASS (or OVERRIDDEN with logged reason).
- **SC-010** *(Deferred)*: Personal donor balance (Model B) funds a recipient's own housing through the escrow chain with a `DonorLedger` debit in place of (or alongside) the `HousingPool` debit. *(Deferred to a follow-up spec; not a gating criterion for `003`.)*
- **SC-011** *(Deferred)*: Direct-payment-plus-commission (Model C) records a buyer's direct payment, applies platform commission, and routes the remainder through the escrow chain. *(Deferred to a follow-up spec; not a gating criterion for `003`.)*
